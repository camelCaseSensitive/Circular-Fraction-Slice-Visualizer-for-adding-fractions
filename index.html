Fraction Addition & Subtraction

Enter two starting fractions.

Enter to begin

Clean circular-slices version. Press ? for controls.

Controls

Hold ← + 2–9
multiply the left fraction by n/n
Hold → + 2–9
multiply the right fraction by n/n
Hold Shift + ←/→ + 2–9
divide that fraction by n/n when possible
Enter or Space
show / hide the result when denominators match
Z
undo the last fraction change
R
reset both fractions
F
choose a new pair of fractions
? or Esc
close this help

Example: hold the left arrow and tap 3 to turn 1/2 into 3/6. Hold the right arrow and tap 2 to turn 1/3 into 2/6.
